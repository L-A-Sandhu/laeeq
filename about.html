Wind-speed estimation, drone telemetry, and physics-informed AI

Laeeq Aslam · Postdoctoral Research Fellow, GTIIT

I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Guangdong Technion–Israel Institute of Technology (GTIIT), where I work on wind-speed estimation using drone telemetry data. My research combines multirotor telemetry, physical modeling, and data-driven inference to recover wind information under real flight conditions.

In parallel, I develop physics-informed machine-learning systems for wind, energy, and climate applications under real hardware limits, including edge-form deployment. The broader goal is to build models that remain physically credible, data efficient, and operationally useful in high-impact regimes.

This work aligns with Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) 7 through better wind characterization for energy systems, SDG 11 through resilient environmental sensing, and SDG 13 through adaptation-oriented atmospheric monitoring and forecasting.

Collaboration / funding interest

I am currently open to collaborations, visiting researcher invitations, and funded projects focused on:

  • Wind speed and load forecasting for grid reliability and renewables integration (SDG 7).
  • Urban heat-risk early warning and climate stress monitoring for cities and health agencies (SDG 11).
  • Deployable AI for climate adaptation and resilience planning under extreme conditions (SDG 13).
